# Package adoption review

This record preserves the manual findings for package-template version 1.0.0. The linked packages are sealed under `packages.0.0.1.legacy/`.
The tables describe that historical scope, including its unresolved classifications and verification limits. They do not establish current package adoption, business acceptance or system acceptance.

At that review, every marked package received a complete tree check and a review of its public responsibility, applicable integration rules and existing behavior owner.
Unresolved architectural classifications remained visible in scan output. They were not automatically violations or acceptance evidence.
The review retained legacy behavior and verification sources pending migration of their consumers and evidence duties.
Current contracts follow their actual owners and the [architecture adoption rules](../../../../docs/architecture/README.md#采用状态与当前有效源). Retaining this record does not grant current authority to conflicting legacy designs.
The current adoption marker belongs to each active package.json. Source or template changes require a new scan under the applicable template version.
