In the last two episodes you saw Justin and I using agents to build some simple applications. Episode 1 started with the basics. Episode 2 began to show the power of acceptance tests and multiple agents.

In this episode, we take it up another notch. The application is much more complex, and we will use four agents, working collaboratively, to build this application right before your eyes. This episode focuses on building and managing the implementation plan, and monitoring the agents as they race through it. I think you’ll be surprised at just how powerful the demonstrated approach is.

And yet, there are many more surprises to come in the episodes to follow. With great power comes great responsibility.
